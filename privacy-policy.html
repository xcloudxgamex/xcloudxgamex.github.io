# Privacy Policy for Pocket Echo: Games **Effective Date:** October 7, 2025 This Privacy Policy explains how **Pocket Echo: Games** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you use our mobile application. --- ### 1. Information Collection and Use We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, physical address, or phone number) directly through the game. However, to provide services, game functionality, and advertising, our application integrates third-party services that may collect device identifiers and usage data. --- ### 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising Our game integrates **Google AdMob** to display personalized and non-personalized advertisements. Third-party ad partners (including Google AdMob) may collect and use information such as: - Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / GAID) - IP address - Coarse location data - Game interaction and ad performance metrics These third parties process data in accordance with their own privacy policies: - **Google Play Services & AdMob:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) --- ### 3. European Rights (GDPR) & Consent Management If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, you have specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Before serving advertisements in these regions, our application presents a **Consent Management Platform (CMP)** dialog powered by Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can grant, customize, or revoke your consent for data collection and targeted advertising at any time within the game settings. --- ### 4. US State Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA) If you are a resident of California or other US states with applicable privacy regulations, you have the right to: - Request disclosure of personal data collected. - Opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted behavioral advertising. You can manage your targeted advertising preferences through your device settings (e.g., *Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete Advertising ID*). --- ### 5. Children's Privacy Our application is not intentionally directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective action. --- ### 6.
